PROJECT WRITE-UP / NETWORK SECURITY

Network Reconnaissance with Nmap

An authorized lab project using Nmap to identify live hosts, open ports and exposed services for defensive network understanding.

← Back to projects

Objective

Build a basic network map in an authorized environment and understand what information an exposed service reveals.

Lab Approach

  • Identified reachable hosts in the lab network.
  • Reviewed open TCP ports and service information.
  • Compared basic scanning with service/version detection.
  • Documented why unnecessary services increase attack surface.

What I Learned

Nmap results become more useful when interpreted in context: a port number alone is less important than the service, version, exposure and business need behind it.

Defensive Takeaways

  • Close unnecessary ports and services.
  • Restrict management interfaces.
  • Patch exposed services.
  • Use network segmentation and firewall rules.
This write-up documents practice performed only in an intentionally vulnerable or authorized lab environment.
